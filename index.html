ABSTRACT

Smart Campus 360 is a web-based student campus management platform designed to provide a centralized, efficient, and user-friendly digital environment for managing essential academic and campus-related activities. The project addresses the inconvenience of accessing information from multiple disconnected sources by bringing important student services together within a single platform. The system provides modules for student login and profile management, attendance tracking, timetable management, assignment monitoring, campus notices, campus mapping, bus routes, scholarships, and placement preparation.

The application is developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a responsive interface designed to provide a consistent experience across desktop and mobile screen sizes. The system uses browser-based local storage to maintain student profile information, attendance records, timetable preferences, assignments, notices, registrations, and placement-related data. The dashboard provides an organized overview of important academic information, including overall attendance, pending assignments, upcoming classes, and recent notices. Subject-wise attendance monitoring allows students to identify their academic status, while the timetable module provides section-specific weekly schedules. Additional modules enable students to explore campus information through college and master-plan images, search bus routes, review scholarship opportunities, and monitor placement preparation skills and events.

The proposed system demonstrates how a centralized digital platform can improve the accessibility, organization, and management of routine student information. By integrating academic tracking, campus services, financial-support information, and career-oriented resources into one interface, Smart Campus 360 provides a practical foundation for a digitally connected campus environment.

The project can be further enhanced in the future by integrating a backend database, real-time college notifications, cloud authentication, automated attendance synchronization, online assignment submission, and personalized analytics.
